The Essentials

Six things your site is probably missing

These aren't nice-to-haves. They're the difference between "meh" and "whoa."

Custom Cursor

Move your mouse right now. That little dot following you? That's personality. Stock cursors feel like a sterile waiting room.

Scroll Reveals

Elements that fade in as you scroll aren't just pretty — they create rhythm. They guide the eye and reward exploration.

Magnetic Buttons

Buttons that pull toward your cursor feel alive. It's a tiny detail that silently screams "this was crafted, not coded."

Theme Toggle

Dark mode isn't optional anymore. The toggle itself should feel satisfying — a micro-moment of control.

Living Shapes

Static geometry is dead. Blobs that morph, rotate, and breathe give your page an organic heartbeat.

Instant Feedback

Every click, hover, and interaction needs a response under 100ms. No excuses. No loading spinners of shame.
