Capabilities

Crafted with precision, animated with life

Every surface we design responds to its environment — light, motion, and context shape the experience in real time.

Fluid Materials
Surfaces that bend, refract, and flow — not static rectangles, but living materials that respond to interaction.
Light Simulation
Real-time caustics, prismatic dispersion, and specular highlights that make digital glass indistinguishable from the physical.
Tactile Response
Every hover, press, and gesture produces physical feedback — ripples, distortions, and elastic rebounds.
60fps Always
GPU-accelerated compositing and minimal paint operations ensure buttery smoothness on every device.
Layered Depth
Multiple glass surfaces at different depths create parallax and dimensionality impossible with flat design.
Adaptive Tint
Glass opacity and color shift based on scroll position and content behind — always readable, always beautiful.
