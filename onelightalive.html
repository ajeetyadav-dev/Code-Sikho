Anatomy

Three Parts, One Effect

A single spotlight is deceptively simple. Under the surface, three systems work in harmony.

Reactive Illumination

A radial light source tracks your presence in real-time, casting warm amber across every surface it touches. The darkness isn't empty — it's waiting.

Proximity Awareness

Every element on this page calculates its distance from the light. Cards glow, borders warm, icons ignite — each response proportional to how close you are.

Organic Motion

Linear interpolation with a low factor creates a gentle lag. The light doesn't teleport — it drifts, breathes, and feels alive in a way instant tracking never could.

The difference between a page that exists and a page that lives isn't complexity. It's response.
One signal that says: I see you.

We obsess over adding more — more animations, more effects, more elements. But the most powerful interfaces do less. They listen.
