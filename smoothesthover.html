The Secret

Why these feel different

Not transitions

CSS transitions move between two states. Springs move toward a target — with velocity, momentum, and natural deceleration. The difference is the difference between a sliding door and a swinging one.

Not animation

Keyframe animations follow a timeline. Springs follow physics. If you interrupt a transition halfway, it jankily reverses. Interrupt a spring, and it simply redirects — because it was never following a path, it was chasing a target.

Not preset

Every cubic-bezier curve is a guess at what "smooth" means. A spring doesn't guess — it computes. Stiffness controls speed. Damping controls bounce. Two numbers that describe every motion you've ever felt in your hand.

Physics doesn't lie.

Presets do.

Spring physics: velocity += (target - current) × stiffness; velocity *= damping; current += velocity

That's the whole secret.
